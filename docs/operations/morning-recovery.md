# Morning recovery and operations center

The Morning ledger tracks verified issuance, check registration, bank linking and debt allocation separately. Orders Storage V2 owns debt changes. Its database transaction records durable allocation evidence in the Morning ledger.

## Recovery behavior

- **Check issuance status** reconciles an uncertain issuance with Morning using reads. The operation attempt claim is owner- and environment-scoped. A stale provider response cannot publish after a newer attempt has claimed the operation.
- A verified check import can retry immediately under the service-only RPC with a 30-second per-operation cooldown. Cron uses the same writer and skips locked records. Contention is retried after a minute. Transient and unexpected failures back off and stop after five failed attempts. Authorization and validation failures stop automatic attempts on the first classified failure. An older `mfa_required` failure from before classification becomes eligible for the corrected writer while retaining its attempt count.
- In either site, Settings shows delayed verification, check evidence, check registration and bank linking from the owner’s cloud ledger. The fixed incident control shows active work, repeated list failures, offline status or authentication that needs attention. A single transient list failure appears in Settings while the browser retries automatically. Acknowledgement is scoped to the current tab session.
- The center fetches fifty rows per page and uses the next cursor to reach older operations. Search accepts a document number or exact operation UUID. History covers bank links, check registrations and debt receipts completed within fourteen days. A successful active-list poll refreshes one visible filtered page using its existing cursor. Slower responses for a previous filter or account are discarded.
- The manual retry receipt distinguishes a registered result, an already-completed import, lock contention, cooldown and a saved failure. An Orders manual request waits for any in-flight automatic recovery and runs the requested retry when check registration still needs it. Pending debt decisions and failed durable writes use persistent notices. Acknowledging a notice does not change the ledger or complete the operation.
- The Morning list has its own foreground refresh. It runs every 45 seconds when healthy, retries a failed read after four seconds and backs off after continued failures. It honors server retry deadlines. Reopening a foreground tab or reconnecting resumes verification. An empty Search and the automatic refresh share one account-scoped read. A read deadline covers both the network request and response body. A failed refresh leaves previously reported operations visible in that tab.
- A new debt-linked issuance reserves the operation ID, debt ID, two allocation choices and a five-field financial snapshot before any provider POST. The browser requires an explicit reservation receipt. Its local checkpoint still protects an interrupted issue or a locally queued save. Older already-issued operations without shared choices can resume from their original checkpoint.
- The owner can resume a verified shared checkpoint in Orders from another device, subject to MFA and current Orders write authority. Recovery refreshes the canonical V2 head and asks for new choices if the debt changed. Kupa shows the same pending step and can direct the operator to Orders.
- Completion evidence records each component's outcome and exact amount. A selected component needs its expected active movement; opt-outs and a zero remaining balance need no movement. The operation uses the agreed snapshot and the current balance under the Orders head lock. A competing allocation that changes this snapshot requires review. A later authorized reversal leaves the original completion evidence intact. Receipts issued before this check are treated as historical and are never replayed automatically.

## Background jobs

- The two financial Cron jobs record a heartbeat after the queue runner finishes, even when the queue is empty. The center reports missing or disabled jobs after a five-minute startup window, and reports stalled business work separately. Heartbeats record scheduler completion. Owner-scoped queue evidence records business progress and stopped work.
- Bank source health uses existing accepted snapshots and stored bank history. It reports missing snapshots for accounts with observed history and flags a stored source after 72 hours without advances. An account with no stored evidence remains in the no-history state. Connector configuration stored on an individual device is unavailable to this account-level check. An empty bank queue, a completed evaluation and a changed check match are recorded separately.
- Queue contention defers the same bank item by one minute while retaining its original queue age and failure streak. A verified check import reports missing provider evidence separately from the work eligible for Cron.
- The bank matching worker preserves the oldest pending timestamp and retries transient failures with bounded exponential backoff. A new stored input or the existing authenticated refresh can resume matching. A failed batch retains its queue item while other owners continue. Only safe failure categories and timestamps reach the center.
- Morning keeps historical failure totals independently from the current automatic retry budget. Legacy failures before the classified policy keep their totals and can use the bounded retry window. An operator with current MFA can retry one eligible operation through the existing center. The operation lock, verified intent and manual cooldown apply.
- The browser requests job evidence on the existing first-page poll only. Search, pagination and per-operation retries use their current requests. Downloaded diagnostics contain operation identifiers, safe error codes and stage evidence. They omit provider credentials and payment details.
- Payment mismatches, stopped check registration, pending debt decisions, and verification uncertainty lasting ten minutes use persistent notices. Stalled jobs and repeated monitoring outages do the same. Critical Storage V2 and Calendar incidents reuse the existing incident registry and notice surface. Bank and credit connectors retain their feed diagnostics. Morning expense intake retains its own upload and reconciliation records. None of those browser-driven routes gains a Cron heartbeat.

## Verification

The PostgreSQL fixture covers AAL1 denial, AAL2 access, service-only RPC grants, atomic rollback, historical retry with 64 failures and eligibility before its original schedule, exact ten-check registration, and owner/environment isolation. The browser fixture uses independent Orders and Kupa profiles to exercise discovery, acknowledgement, one retry and convergence. The staging project uses synthetic data and its own provider configuration.
